About

We are an ice cream shop wanting to make people happy, smile, bring good vibes, and bring sunshine into one's day! We offer a wide variety of options including homemade bubble tea, popsicles, ice cream bars, smoothies, sorbets, frozen yogurt, and cake.

We are the best ice cream shop in Florida because we are unique, provide the best quality, and our ingredients are all fairly traded and sourced. We have also been featured in publications such as The New York Times, Bon Appétit, TIME, and Forbes, in addition to being named the best ice cream shop in Florida by WCVB 5.

We provide the best service because we truly care about our customers and the environment. Our waste is converted into electricity powering machines and lights. One percent of our profits goes toward providing school supplies to children who are less fortunate.
